What owners say.

Questions, answered.

Do I need a new number or the WhatsApp Business API?

No. You link the number you already use, the same way WhatsApp Web works. Your chats and contacts stay where they are.

Can I connect more than one WhatsApp number?

Yes. Basic includes 1 number, Standard 3, Pro 5 and Ultra unlimited. Link each number with a QR code, give it a name like Sales or Support, and switch between numbers from the sidebar. Your team and AI setup are shared across all of them, and on Ultra you can see analytics for each number separately.

Will the AI reply to my friends and family?

No. People saved in your phone are skipped by default and you can switch the AI off for any chat. It also reads the chat first: if you've already answered, it doesn't reply again. You choose whether it steps back completely once you reply yourself.

Which languages does it speak?

It replies in whatever language your customer writes or speaks, including Arabic, Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Tamil, Tagalog, French, Russian and many more, written in their own script. Voice-note replies (Ultra plan) are available in more than 70 languages.

How do voice-note replies in my voice work?

On the Ultra plan, the owner records a short sample of their own voice in the dashboard. When a customer sends a voice note, the AI replies with a voice note that sounds like you, in the customer's language. You can turn it off or delete your voice at any time. Only your own voice can be used.

What if a customer asks something the AI doesn't know?

It only uses what you've told it about your business. For anything else it says it will check, and you get a WhatsApp alert so you can step in.

Is WhatsEaze an official WhatsApp product?

No. WhatsEaze is an independent service that connects to your number as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. WhatsApp's own rules still apply, so use it to talk to people who want to hear from you.

Who can see my chats?

Your team, according to the roles you give them, and authorised WhatsEaze staff only when needed for support or security. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.

Does it tell people they're talking to an AI?

If someone sincerely asks, it says it's an assistant replying for you. It never pretends otherwise.

Is bulk messaging safe for my number?

WhatsEaze sends one message at a time with natural gaps and a daily limit, and skips anyone who opted out. Only message people who know you; sending to cold lists can still get any number blocked by WhatsApp.

How does the free trial work?

Pick the plan you want and add your card at checkout. For 3 days you get everything in Ultra, whichever plan you picked, and pay nothing. If you keep it, your card is charged when the trial ends; cancel from the Billing page any time before and you're never charged.

Which currency will I pay in?

Prices are shown in your local currency where we can, and you pay in that currency at checkout. Taxes are added where they apply.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the Billing page and you keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.
